Different personality types handle different modes of learning differently. Some students expect order and are well organized. These students struggle with the unpredictability of group discussions ([[@1991copepowell_foreign#^341f7d|Cope Powell, 1991]]) but likely do well on paper exams. Conversely, students who are more outgoing may do well in group activities but struggle with organizing their work and taking exams.
