Rational emotive therapy is practice of using logical thinking to combat negative self-talk. This model of therapy assumes that the negative thinking around a situation is based on limited or no experience ([[@1991foss_relational#^b17949|Foss & Reitzel, 1991, p. 135, citing Grieger & Boyd, 1980]]). It can be effective in treating apprehension where inexperience, lack of confidence, or traumatic previous experience is the main cause.
