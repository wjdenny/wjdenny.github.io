Watching videos with subtitles can have a number of useful benefits, including:
- being able to watch content without disturbing others nearby,
- making it easier to translate content from one language to another, 
- making the video content searchable,
- syncing video timestamps with content, and
- providing comprehensible input for language learning.

This makes it an excellent example of [[The curb-cut effect is the way that changes made to benefit marginalized people also benefits everyone else.|the curb-cut effect]].
