Academic skills transfer when new languages are acquired. The cognitive skills used in academic work is the same regardless of the languages ([[@2017choudhury_begali#^3e8571|Choudhury & Garrison-Fletcher, 2017, quoting Cummins, 2005]]). Refugee and immigrant students with interrupted formal education experiences often struggle with these academic skills in addition to language barriers which makes it difficult to find success in U.S. schools ([[@2016hos_lives#^997d27|Hos , 2016, citing Carholl, Sua'rez-Orozco, & Pa'ez, 2008]]).
