Not all linguistic minority students are the same. We cannot conflate first-generation immigrant, refugee students, second- or third- generation students or international students without proper research ([[@2010kanno_immigrant|Kanno & Varghese, 2010]]). Hos ([[@2016hos_lives#^1efa94|2016]]) reports that immigrant students advance much faster through a secondary school "newcomer program" than do refugee students, likely as a result of a history of interrupted formal education.
