Learning anxieties act as a vocational filter for the people most likely affected by them. For example, science anxiety and math anxiety tend to affect women as well as Black and Hispanic people the most. This limits their ability to success in those degree programs and the high-paying jobs available to those graduates ([[@1991campbell_helping#^b09e74|Campbell & Ortiz, 1991, citing Mallow, 1981 and Sells 1978]]).
