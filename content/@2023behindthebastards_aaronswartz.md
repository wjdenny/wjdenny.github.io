## Notes


- [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=677#t=11:16.95) [[Aaron Swartz]] dropped out of school after 8th grade and took college courses. 
- [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=724#t=12:03.54) [[Aaron Swartz]] was an autodidact.
- [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=754#t=12:34.33) [[Aaron Swartz]] was good at teaching himself, but lacked discipline and staying power.
- [14:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=882#t=14:42.37) [[Potatoes are the real Gold of El Dorado.]] ^c5edf4
- [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=943#t=15:42.89) In 1999, age 12, [[Aaron Swartz]] created the InfoNetwork, a precursor to Wikipedia.
- [17:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=1044#t=17:23.76) He was a finalist for the Ars Digita prize. The Ars Digita prize recognizes the achievements of young people who create valuable non-commercial websites.
- [18:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=1091#t=18:11.24) [[Aaron Swartz]] found a way into the W3C as a delegate for the HTML Writers Guild.
- [20:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=1236#t=20:36.28) [[Understanding how bureaucratic systems work is a valuable skill.]] ^db7f44
- [23:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=1438#t=23:58.48) In 2000, [[Aaron Swartz]] began working on the development of RSS.
- [29:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=1769#t=29:28.62) [[Tim Burners Lee]], an MIT professor who invented the World Wide Web, took [[Aaron Swartz]] in as a protégé.
- [30:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=1852#t=30:52.32) [[Tim Burners Lee]] deliberately chose not to patent the World Wide Web to keep it open.
- [33:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=1987#t=33:06.64) At age 15, [[Aaron Swartz]] started working with [[Lawrence Lessig]], a lawyer who created the [[Creative Commons]] license.
- [34:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=2088#t=34:48.11) [[Lawrence Lessig]] knew early on that the Internet could be corrupted by authority. Most of the community had a techno-optimist perspective.
- [35:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=2134#t=35:34.12) [Code and Other Laws of Cyberspace](https://www.amazon.com/Code-Other-Cyberspace-Lawrence-Lessig/dp/046503912X), book by [[Lawrence Lessig]]; programmers will have more influence than laws on the development of the Internet; laws have to be enforced and processed, but programmers can make actions impossible.
- [37:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=2279#t=37:59.44) The tension between Internet-as-freedom and Internet-as-authority communities has existed as long as the Internet has existed. There is potential for there to be a complete split in the Internet because of this.
- [41:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=2474#t=41:13.91) The expectation that the adults around [[Aaron Swartz]] had for him likely put a lot of stress on him, and led to feeling responsibility for the future of the Internet.
- [42:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=2556#t=42:36.17) [[Aaron Swartz]] was a prolific blogger and preferred writing to coding.
- [44:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=2694#t=44:54.14) [[Aaron Swartz]] started Infogami, which later absorbed into Reddit.
- [48:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=2906#t=48:26.48) Conde Nast bought Reddit. 
- [50:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=3028#t=50:27.93) The sale of Reddit leads to disagreements between the new leadership and [[Aaron Swartz]], mainly around its design focused on user attention and time.
- [55:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-rv7uBJCg8&t=3304#t=55:04.03) [[Aaron Swartz]] created [[The Open Library]] with Brewster Kahl, Alexis Rossi, Anand Chitipothu, and Rebecca Malamud. The goal being to collect every book in the world and digitize it or at least have information about it.
