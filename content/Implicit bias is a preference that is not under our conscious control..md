Implicit bias is a preference that is not under our conscious control. This is in contrast to explicit bias or conscious bias. Implicit bias is unconscious[^1] which means that it exists outside of our level of awareness. Implicit bias is cultural programming that we get from our family, society, and other social groups[^2]. Implicit biases are not necessarily ideas that we even agree with and often surface in times of stress[^3]. [[Teachers can do a lot of damage if implicit biases are unchecked.]]
