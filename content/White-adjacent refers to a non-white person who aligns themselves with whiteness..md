White-adjacent refers to a non-white person who aligns themselves with whiteness. In this process, they also distance themselves from their ethnic group ([[@2023gassamasare_decentering_dei#^6dc98c|Gassam Asare, 2023, p. 78]]). Examples of this may include the colorism that is prevalent in East and South Asia, though colorism also has its own history and etiology independent of whiteness and colonialism.
