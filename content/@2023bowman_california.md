## Notes
> California Gov. Gavin Newsom has announced a new contract with nonprofit drugmaker Civica Rx, a move that brings the state one step closer to creating its own line of insulin to bring down the cost of the drug.
>
> The state-label insulins [will cost no more than $30](https://californiahealthline.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2022/05/Civica-Affordable-Insulin-Press-Release-March-2022.pdf) per 10 milliliter vial, and no more than $55 for a box of five pre-filled pen cartridges — for both insured and uninsured patients. The medicines will be [available nationwide](https://twitter.com/GavinNewsom/status/1637162028610752512?s=20), the governor's office said. #pharmaceutics

> A 10 milliliter vial of insulin can cost as much as $300, Newsom said. Under the new contract, patients who pay out of pocket for insulin could save up to $4,000 per year. The federal government this year put a [$35 monthly cap on out-of-pocket costs on insulin](https://www.npr.org/2023/03/14/1163354744/insulin-price-cuts-novo-nordisk-diabetes) for certain Medicare enrollees, including senior citizens. #disability #healthcare #poverty #gerontology #medicare
>
> The news comes after a handful of drugmakers that dominate the insulin market recently said they would cut the list prices of their insulin. (List prices, set by the drugmaker, are often what uninsured patients — or those with high deductibles — must pay for the drug out-of-pocket.) #pharmaceutics #disability

> After rival Eli Lilly announced a plan to [slash the prices of some of its insulin](https://www.npr.org/2023/03/01/1160339792/eli-lilly-insulin-price) by 70%, Novo Nordisk and Sanofi [followed suit this past week](https://www.npr.org/2023/03/14/1163354744/insulin-price-cuts-novo-nordisk-diabetes), saying they would lower some list prices for some of their insulin products by as much 75% next year. Together, the three companies [control some 90%](https://www.npr.org/2023/03/18/1164523151/two-more-drug-companies-joined-eli-lilly-in-lowering-the-cost-of-insulin) of the U.S. insulin supply. #pharmaceutics #disability 

> Insulin is a critical drug for people with Type 1 diabetes, whose body doesn't produce enough insulin. People with Type 1 need insulin daily in order to survive. #disability 

> The insulin contract is part of California's broader [CalRx initiative](https://calrx.ca.gov/) to produce generic drugs under the state's own label. Newsom says the state is pushing to manufacture generic [naloxone](https://www.npr.org/2023/02/18/1157556969/narcan-fda-over-the-counter) next. #pharmaceutics #disability #addiction
