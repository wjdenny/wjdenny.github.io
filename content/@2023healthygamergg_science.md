## Notes
It's possible to catch up in life. There's no such thing as "missing the boat."
We often have limiting beliefs on our ability to catch up in life.

[2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=2m2s) Catch up growth: Normal eating after periods of famine causes organisms to grow faster, catching up with their peers.

[3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=3m18s) Attachment and behavioral catch up: Similar to catch up growth, people who do not form healthy attachments during critical attachment periods can still do so after.

[5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=5m) The most growth occurs at the most challenging point. (i.e., last two reps of a weight lift)

[6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=6m45s) Minimal assistance during challenging points offers significant benefits.
- One hour of therapy per week
- A friend helping you cook once a week
- A friend helping you with the last rep of a bench press.
- A friend playing a sport game (racquetball) once a week

Another benefit of these is the added social pressure and accountability.

[9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=9m) The environment a person is in influences their behaviors.
- This echoes others' idea of "you are the people you spend the most time with"

[10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=10m38s) Make eye contact with someone in the group and smile; this helps you insert yourself into a group.
Ask a few open ended questions related to what they're discussing.

[13:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=13m10s) change your physical environment.
This is why I naturally want to go to coffee shops and libraries to work. Shift in your environment can change your focus.

[14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=14m45s) How our brains sabotage our efforts to catch up
our emotions take control / negative self-talk

He says we can identify whether a thought is emotional or logical because emotional thoughts will always lead to you not doing the thing; but logical thoughts should offer different solutions.

Meditation and noticing helps to decompress emotions
Shame is behind it all. Believes all action will lead to failure.

The brain believes that any efforts will lead to failure and tries to protect us from experiencing repeated failures which is why we experience fear and anxiety anticipating those efforts.

[19:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IOWelVx5CUw?t=19m49s) Watch out for I statements. When you start to believe something about yourself, it starts to change what your behavior. Socially anxious? avoid parties. It's the other way around; your identity is shaped by your behaviors. You can still be socially anxious but do social activities anyway.
