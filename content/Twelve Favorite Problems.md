
I encountered this idea from Tiago Forte's [[@2022forte_second|Building a Second Brain]] (2022), citing Richard Feynman[^1]. They suggest developing a list of twelve favorite problems or questions to 

1. How can I help adolescents struggling with mental health and identity?
2. How can I prepare my community for climate and political crises?
3. How can I design courses that maximizes positive impacts on students?
4. How can I advocate for systemic changes that builds equity for marginalized people?
5. How can I decenter intersecting systems of privilege in my work and life?
6. How can I work to change culture in a way that respects those with antithetical views?
7. How can I support trans-nationalism and cultural diversity in my communities?
8. How can I communicate better in speaking for both public and academic audiences?
9. How can I communicate better in writing for both public and academic audiences?
10. How can I improve the organization and function of my research notes?
11. How can I develop habits to make myself a better teacher, therapist, and partner?
12. What can history teach us about the social problems we face today?
