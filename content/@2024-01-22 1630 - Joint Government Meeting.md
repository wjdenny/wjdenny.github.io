### Public Comments
[00:03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=182#t=03:02.20) Nix spoke during public comments criticizing the lack of awareness of the root causes of violence in our communities. They specifically mentioned the lack of awareness of social and economic factors that place Black community members at higher risks of violence, as well as the arrest and surveillance of trans protesters.

[00:06:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=362#t=06:02.15) Tamra Raker spoke about the lack of mental heath care leading to violence and encounters with law enforcement.

[00:08:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=500#t=08:20.46) Kate spoke to echo the concerns about labeling of certain communities as "hot spots". Kate argued that the interventions should not be trying to help people without getting the affected community's input on how they would like to be helped.

The members of the meeting seemed to basically ignore the public comments. The way public comments are handled in these meetings limits the total time for public comments to 10 minutes. Elected members of local government have no limits on the time they have for questions. Elected members have a responsibility to center the public comments in their question time.

### Trip Connect
[00:34:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2052#t=34:12.20) Trip Connect is a demand-response transportation pilot in northern Johnson County. It supports people with disabilities or recently incarcerated folks to get them to work or school and back.

Planning and coordination
- NCMM Community Mobility Design Challenge and Learning Launch Grant 2021
- Seats Same Day On-Demand Service launches August 2022
	- [00:36:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2179#t=36:18.52) using American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funding
- Employment Transportation Summit August 15, 2023
	- [00:36:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2193#t=36:32.54)  Summit to discuss transportation needs; elected officials, industry, and human services attended.

[00:35:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2106#t=35:06.49) [[Kelly Schneider]] stated that one of the most prominent barriers to transportation in her work has been in getting to and from job sites.

Partnering with employers to get their workers to and from work sites. It does not seem to be a general purpose transportation support for people. It is support for industry. I understand they want industry input to keep the service funded, but it would be important to have something like a Community Transit Committee involved to prevent industry needs from dominating what *should* be public infrastructure.

![[Pasted image 20240402161132.png]]

[00:38:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2311#t=38:30.98)  20 "ESL or HiSET" students from Kirkwood contacted [[Kelly Schneider]] directly to request help. Kirkwood's transfer of the Iowa City campus disrupted transportation for students; what was a 25 minute frequency route now 65 minutes with 3 bus transfers does not connect to Coralville or Iowa City transit buses. Kirkwood contributed 10,000 USD for the first year of the Trip Connect project ([00:49:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2941#t=49:01.26)).

[00:42:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2536#t=42:16.09)  CorridorRides ECICOG receives federal transit dollars in our area.

[00:44:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2667#t=44:26.94) Service area includes North Liberty and Coralville. Riders must either start or end their trip in the service area and the entire trip must be within Johnson County.

[00:45:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2732#t=45:32.06) Proposed service hours is designed to wrap around SEATS service, including:
- 22:00--23:00 Sunday
- 07:00--20:30 Monday through Friday
- 17:30--12:30 Monday through Friday

[00:46:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=2806#t=46:45.53) Fare is planned as 2.00 USD for the rider. They are asking employers to buy in as well. Attitudes and willingness among employers varies.

[00:54:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3244#t=54:03.87)  National Center for Mobility Management (NCMM) grant involves a working group to analyze data on how Trip Connect influences the economy and employment rates of the region.

[00:55:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3308#t=55:08.10) The ARPA pilot discovered that riders "phased out" of the service because they eventually no longer needed it. This might suggest that they view this as a temporary assistance for people until they get their own private car; rather than an alternative infrastructure to private vehicles.

[00:56:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3407#t=56:46.59) [[Mazahir Salih]] asked how riders can access the service. Demand-response services are scheduled in advanced (one day to two weeks). Must be approved for the service. Riders can be approved by the operator, employers, human service agencies, and others make referrals for the service. So, is this means tested? I'm a bit confused about how a rider will get access to this and whether it would require English language and self-advocacy skills.

[00:58:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3513#t=58:32.90) Similar programs include SEATS/ARPA, North Liberty Transit Assistance Program; they are confident that the projected number of riders is a reliable estimate based on requests of those services.

No discussion on the impact transportation programs like this could have on sustainable infrastructure and climate preparedness goals.

### 988 Update
[[Sarah Nelson]], CEO of [[CommUnity]] gave an update of 988.

[00:59:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3590#t=59:50.35)  988 is a number for the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline (NSPL) since July 2022. 

[01:00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3631#t=1:00:31.31) CommUnity has answered for NSPL on phone chat and text since 2012; providing both local and national service for 988.

[01:00:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3654#t=1:00:53.57) You can text to 988. Grown by 160% since July 2022; most favorable way to reach out. You can call or chat online as well.

[01:01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3675#t=1:01:15.39) Since July 2022, 2,500 contacts originating from within Johnson County; 30,000 from the state of Iowa.

[01:01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3688#t=1:01:27.90) CommUnity Crisis Services answers 98% of all chat and texts originating within Iowa.

[01:01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3703#t=1:01:43.03) CommUnity partners with Foundation 2 in Cedar Rapids to provide these services in Iowa.

[01:01:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3716#t=1:01:56.05) Vibrant oversees the national contract[^1]. In July, 2022 there was a shift to have national contracts back up state services. 

[01:02:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3749#t=1:02:29.37) CommUnity answers the LGBTQ 988 sub-network. Other sub-networks include Spanish and Veterans.

[01:03:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3782#t=1:03:02.00) The push is for less national centers and more state-funded centers. Sarah argues that these local-first services are more equipped to inform callers about local services and resources.

[01:03:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3833#t=1:03:53.50) 18% increase in phone and 160% increase in chat and text since July 2022. People are moving to a preference for chat and text.

[01:04:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3860#t=1:04:19.95) 97% of 988 calls are de-escalated and stabilized on contact.

[01:04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3894#t=1:04:54.38) Highest risks of suicide are post-hospitalization and after discharge from a substance use facility. People don't necessarily feel better right away and end up feeling more hopeless.

[01:05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=3959#t=1:05:59.29) There is a pilot program in Iowa to divert calls from 911 to 988 in Johnson, Muscatine, and Blackhawk county. So far only a few calls have been diverted. Sarah believes the state of Iowa did not want to invest training employees to divert calls.

[01:08:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=4103#t=1:08:22.82) Data analysis is difficult on crisis calls because anonymity is very important. The LGBTQ sub-network is specifically for LGBTQ youth. It is absolutely appropriate to recommend calling or texting 988 for youth experiencing crisis. Sarah estimates that youth are significant users of 988 due to the number of text and chat contacts.

[01:11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=4266#t=1:11:06.43) Even if the state does not want to invest in having crisis managers in the call centers, local boards can decide to do this.

[01:11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=4287#t=1:11:27.04) If a contact results in in-person response, they do what is called a "warm hand-off" where they stay on the line while getting the mobile crisis team in the area to the location. Her goal is to allow the 988 counselor to send a mobile crisis team directly when warranted. 

[01:12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omMG9NsLdpY&t=4356#t=1:12:36.15) There is a HHS realignment happening state-wide, crisis continuum study; everything we know about crisis services is about to change dramatically in the next few years. Currently, services are very inconsistent.
