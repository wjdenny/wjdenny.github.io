Mistakes can be opportunities. Some mistakes, of course, serve no purpose and others can even be career-ending. However, some mistakes can be opportunities. Gian-Carlo Rota ([[@1997rota_ten#^e1f9b8|1997]]) gave similar advice after reflecting on times he and colleagues have written articles riddled with mistakes, which only served to improve and strengthen the theory being proposed.
