The use of a "free space" can be used to allow students to avoid being called on during hard days. Jo Ann Cope Powell ([[@1991copepowell_foreign#^6761f4|1991]]) suggests giving students this opportunity to use on days when they are experiencing significant anxiety or difficulty in the class. It is important to monitor students who use this space. If a particular student is using it often, it is also a good idea to talk to them privately to see what the issue is.
