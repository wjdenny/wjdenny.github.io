The Diversity Visa Lottery program drains vulnerable countries of quality human resources.

Eligibility requirements of the program include having completed high school or two years of vocational training ([[@2012hailu_lived#^cc6096|Hailu et al., 2012, citing U.S. Department of State, 1997]]).

When visa recipients return to their home country for travel, their relative wealth encourages further emigration ([[@2012hailu_lived#^a22edc|Hailu et al., 2012, citing Fussell, 2010]]) and politically destabilizes the country ([[@2012hailu_lived#^a22edc|Hailu et al., 2012, citing Maharaji, 2010]]).
