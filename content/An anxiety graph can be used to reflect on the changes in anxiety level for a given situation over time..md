An anxiety graph can be used to reflect on the changes in anxiety level for a given situation over time. It is helpful for showing clients and students that it is not the entire situation that triggers anxiety, but some parts are more or less triggering than others. Efforts can then be made to support them in those specific areas ([[@1991foss_relational#^69bab7|Foss & Reitzel, 1991, p. 136, citing Brownell & Katula, 1984]]).
