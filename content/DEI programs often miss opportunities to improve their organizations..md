Gassam Asare ([[@2023gassamasare_decentering_dei#^f4c47d|2023, p. 87]]) asserts that many companies ignore the "equity" part of the equation. They focus more on racial and ethnic diversity (I would say, also gender), because it is easier to measure ([[@2023gassamasare_decentering_centering#^a640d7|p. 123]]). The result of this would then logically be lagging diversity and inclusion as marginalized people would feel less safe and less motivated to participate. Organizations would be expected to see worse retention for marginalized people as a result.
