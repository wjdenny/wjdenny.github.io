Whiteness is property. [[Race is a social construct.|Race is a social construct]] and, as such, it influences behavior and perception; our own and those around us. Cheryl Harris argued that whiteness and property share a common core feature which is the right to exclude.[^1] She also discusses the history of legal discussions in which whiteness has played a similar role to property. The ability to create a role and confer upon those in that role specific privileges in such subtle moves is one way for [[Aristocracy is prevalent despite democratic reforms.|aristocracy to masquerade as meritocracy]]. Using this right to exclude, [[Whiteness was created to divide the working class.|whiteness has the ability to divide the working class]].

If whiteness is property, and whiteness, like race in general, is socially constructed, then couldn't all racial identity be property?
