Social support should not be used in lieu of efforts to reduce environmental stress. House ([[@1985hirsch_social#^1dc772|1981, cited by Hirsch, 1985]]) cautions against allowing efforts to build social support to replace efforts to reduce stress in the workplace. In the context of linguistic minority students in higher education, faculty and staff can help students build [[Socioacademic Relationships|socioacademic support networks]], but the work of making the institution more accessible still remains.
